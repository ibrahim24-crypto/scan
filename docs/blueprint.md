# **App Name**: AuraLens

## Core Features:

- Interactive Scan Pad: An interactive UI element for users to 'press and hold' their finger, simulating a scanning process.
- Scan Progress Visualizer: Visual feedback, such as a pulsating glow or a progress bar, indicating that the 'scan' is in progress.
- Fixed Compliment Display: Displays a pre-set message to the user, affirming their unlimited beauty, cuteness, a preference for chocolate, favorite color black, and acknowledging their special and rare name.
- Dynamic Message Display: Presents the personalized compliments and attributes in an elegant and engaging manner after the 'scan' is complete.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design emphasizes a sophisticated dark scheme to align with the user's 'favorite color black', while exuding a charming and elegant feel. The primary accent is a rich Royal Violet (#A663E9) which provides a vibrant pop against a Deep Eggplant (#261F2E) background, lending an ethereal and captivating mood. An Electric Blue-Violet (#8686F9) serves as an energetic secondary accent for highlights and interactive elements.
- All text will use 'Belleza', a humanist sans-serif font known for its clean lines and artistic personality, which aligns perfectly with themes of fashion, art, and unique beauty.
- Minimalist icons should be used, primarily focusing on a 'fingerprint' or abstract 'scan' motif for the interactive pad, designed with subtle glows or ethereal textures.
- The layout should be clean and centered, drawing focus to the interactive scanning area initially, then seamlessly transitioning to prominently display the personalized compliment message. Generous use of whitespace will enhance the elegant aesthetic.
- Subtle, smooth animations will enhance user interaction, including a gentle pulse or shimmering effect on the scanning area and an elegant fade-in transition when revealing the personalized message.
